Data and method

Intents in the knowledge base9
Training examples (knowledge base)110
Dev split (threshold only)61 in scope, 10 off topic
Test split (reported numbers)109 in scope, 16 off topic
Leaked phrases (same words, or cosine of 0.95 or more)0
Customer messages simulated928
Messages queued for learning47
LLM calls in this run0
  • The classifier learns only from the knowledge base examples.
  • The dev split chooses the confidence threshold; the test split was frozen and committed before any evaluation code existed, and has only lost phrases since.
  • No test phrase is the same model input as a training or dev phrase: the same words once stopwords are dropped, in any order, or a cosine of 0.95 or more, count as a leak and were removed.
  • Simulated customers draw their messages from the test split and add typos, filler words and order numbers at random, with a fixed seed. They rephrase once when rejected and answer the cancellation question honestly.
  • A conversation counts as resolved only when the bot answered the main question and gave no wrong answer; a wrong answer is a failure even if the right one came later.
